Company Overview

Trusted Engineering Partner

Founded in 1986, S.M.R. Engineering (I) Private Limited has grown into a trusted company delivering high-quality solutions for some of India's most demanding industrial projects.

Our Mission

To provide world-class engineering, fabrication, manufacturing, erection, and commissioning services while maintaining the highest standards of quality, safety, and customer satisfaction.

Our Vision

To become one of India's most trusted engineering companies by delivering innovative, reliable, and sustainable industrial solutions that contribute to national infrastructure.

Our Journey

From early projects with Cimmco Birla Ltd. to executing complex engineering work for organizations like Indian Oil Corporation and Alstom, our journey is defined by technical excellence.
