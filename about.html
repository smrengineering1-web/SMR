Company Overview

Building India's Future Through Engineering

Founded in 1986, S.M.R. Engineering (I) Private Limited has established itself as one of India's trusted engineering companies delivering fabrication, manufacturing, erection and commissioning solutions for large industrial projects.

We execute multi-dimensional government and private projects across Oil & Gas, Railways, Hydro, Power, and Infrastructure sectors with an unyielding commitment to safety and quality.

  • Heavy Fabrication
  • Pressure Vessels
  • Heat Exchangers
  • Railway Wagon Manufacturing
  • Hydro Mechanical Equipment
  • Industrial Piping
  • Steel Fabrication
  • Installation & Commissioning
