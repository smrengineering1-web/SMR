Quick Overview

01. Heavy Structures

Structural & Heavy Fabrication

We are industry leaders in the fabrication of massive industrial structures, heavy-duty tanks, and highly customized steel components. Utilizing advanced welding technologies and strict metallurgical protocols, we ensure structural integrity for the most demanding environments.

  • Custom Steel Components
  • Heavy Industrial Tanks
  • Load-bearing Structures
  • Non-destructive Testing
02. Static Equipment

Pressure Vessels & Heat Exchangers

S.M. Engineering specializes in the fabrication of Pressure Vessels according to strict global industrial quality standards (ASME). Additionally, we manufacture robust shell and tube Heat Exchangers optimized for heavy refinery and chemical plant applications.

  • High-pressure Tolerance
  • Refinery Applications
  • Chemical Processing Units
  • ASME Standard Compliance
03. Hydro Engineering

Hydro Mechanical Equipment

Delivering critical hydro mechanical infrastructure for major dam and water projects. Our expertise covers the complete fabrication, assembly, and erection of massive gates, spillways, and heavy water-control structures built to withstand immense hydraulic forces.

  • Radial & Sluice Gates
  • Spillway Infrastructure
  • Heavy Penstocks
  • Dam Superstructures
04. Railway Infrastructure

Railway Wagon Manufacturing

A flagship capability of S.M.R. Engineering. We provide end-to-end manufacturing and sub-assembly for the Indian railway sector, supported by our advanced in-house tool room and surface treatment facilities.

  • BOXNHL & BCNHL Wagons
  • BOBRN & BLC A&B
  • Complete Sub Assembly
  • Air Brake Arrangement
  • Shot Blasting & Painting
  • Precision Fixtures & Jigs
05. Fluid & Safety Systems

Industrial Piping & Fire Fighting

We execute complex Pipeline Fabrication and installation for Oil & Gas refineries and heavy pump houses. Paired with our expertise in Industrial Fire Protection systems, we ensure safe, fully commissioned pipeline networks for hazardous environments.

  • Oil & Gas Pipelines
  • Refinery Pump Houses
  • Industrial Fire Protection
  • Testing & Commissioning
06. Heavy Machinery

Mechanical & Cement Plant Equipment

Manufacturing heavy mechanical equipment, industrial machinery, and robust structural components. We also specialize in Cement Plant infrastructure including comprehensive material handling systems.

  • Storage & Mix Bed Plants
  • Loading Plants
  • High-capacity Conveyors
  • Bucket Elevators & Feeders
